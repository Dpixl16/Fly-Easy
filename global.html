Global Tips

Pre-Departure. Departure.
In-flight. Arrival.

Every flight breaks down into four phases. Follow the plane to the one you need.

Pre-Departure

Know before you go.

A few minutes of prep at home saves hours of stress at the airport.

Pack it

Bring ID, passport & key documents — and double-check they're actually on you before you leave the house.
Bring your own headphones — cabin jacks rarely fit newer plugs.
Download shows & music offline — don't count on the plane's wifi.
Pack a charger + cable — outlets exist on many flights, but not every seat has one.

Know it

Research entry requirements early — Japan's QR pre-registration, for example, cuts customs time dramatically.
Check your airline's baggage rules — a 2-minute look avoids a gate-side surprise.

Departure

Timing Matters.

The single biggest source of departure stress — and the easiest one to fix once you know the real timeline.

Check in 24 hours early

It opens the moment your airline allows it — skip the counter, lock in your seat, and have your pass ready before you even leave home.

Then, get to the airport

Extra time for document checks

Add 20–30 minutes if you're checking a bag, flying from a busy hub, or departing 6–9am on a weekday.

Early isn't wasted time — it's productive time you get back.

Before you board

💧 Empty it, refill after — security won't let liquids through, but water fountains wait right on the other side.
🧳 Bag drop closes early — checked-bag counters often stop 30–60 minutes before departure, even if you're "on time."
🚻 Go before you board — the aisle gets crowded fast once boarding starts.
🔎 Recheck your gate — it changes after security more than you'd think, right when you've stopped looking.

Cabin comfort

Settle into the flight.

A few small habits make the miles in between feel shorter.

Settle in

Hydrate more than feels normal — cabin air is drier than any room you know.
Sit behind the wing — the smoothest ride, closest to the plane's center of gravity.
Stretch every couple hours — a quick aisle walk or seated stretch keeps circulation moving on longer flights.

Stay aware

Ask the back galley — free snacks & water are available anytime on international flights, not just during service.
Adjust to your arrival timezone — set your watch and sleep schedule the moment you board.
Watch takeoff and landing — the view outside is one of the simplest ways to calm pre-flight nerves.

Plans change

Your itinerary is a plan, not a promise.

Customs and connections rarely run on the schedule you booked.

15 min–2h+
15 min on a quiet day 2+ hrs on a busy one

Customs & immigration timing swings wildly — never book a tight connection right after landing.

Right away

Always have foreign currency ready — cash still rules for taxis, tips, and small vendors before you find an ATM.
Grab an eSIM before landing — activate the moment wheels touch down.

Just in case

Screenshot your hotel address — no signal, no problem for the driver.
Have a backup plan — customs and baggage claim can turn chaotic or take far longer than scheduled.

One more thing

The sky does this every night.

Wherever you land, grab the window seat on the way home too.
