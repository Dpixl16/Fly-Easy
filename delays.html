Delays & cancellations

Delayed? Cancelled? Don't worry.

Above the clouds it's always calm. You'll get there too.

The biggest tip

Be nice and respectful. It gets you further than yelling ever will.

They didn't cause it, and they're the ones who can fix it. Say hello, wait your turn, ask instead of demand, and say thank you.

  • Flight attendants
  • Pilots
  • Gate agents
  • Desk staff
  • Airport staff
  • Phone agents

Breathe, then try this

"This is ridiculous. Fix it!"

"I know this isn't your fault. What are my options?"

"Get me on the next flight. Now."

"I found flight 456 at 6:10. Could you check for a seat?"

"I demand a hotel!"

"Since this is overnight, is a hotel something you can help with?"

Be nice and respectful.

Always check your own airline's policy

This is a general guide, not legal advice. What you get depends on your airline, your ticket and the country, and the rules change. Look up your airline's delay and cancellation policy and confirm it for your flight.

If the first flight ran late, the airline normally puts you on its next available flight for free.

Know your rights

What you're owed

It depends on where you're flying.

1You always get a choice

Flight cancelled, or changed in a big way

Still want to go

Take the new flight

Rebooked at no extra cost. No ticket refund, since you traveled.

Rather not go

Get your money back

Decline the new flight and any voucher. Refund to your original payment, even on a non-refundable ticket: 7 business days for cards, 20 days otherwise.

2What counts as a "big change"

Domestic 3 hours
International 6 hours
02 h4 h6 h8 h

Leaving that much earlier or arriving that much later.

Also counts:A different airportMore connectionsA lower cabin class

3Who caused it matters

The airline's side

Maintenance, crew, cleaning, fuelling, loading bags

  • Refund if you don't travel
  • Free rebooking
  • Meal after a 3-hour wait
  • Overnight hotel (most airlines)

Out of its hands

Weather, air traffic control, security

  • Refund if you don't travel
  • Rebooking on the same airline
  • Meals aren't promised
  • Hotels aren't promised

No US rule makes an airline pay you cash for a delay. Anything beyond a refund comes from promises each airline has made.

What the nine biggest US airlines promise when they cause the problem

  • 9 of 9 rebook you free on their own flights
  • 9 of 9 give a meal or voucher after 3 hours
  • 8 of 9 give a hotel and a ride to it overnight
  • 6 of 9 will rebook you on a partner airline
  • 0 of 9 promise cash for your time

Alaska, Allegiant, American, Delta, Frontier, Hawaiian, JetBlue, Southwest and United. Checked November 2026. See your airline on the US DOT dashboard.

Checked bag late? Report it before you leave the airport. The bag fee comes back if it's 12+ hours late (domestic) or 15 to 30 hours late (international, by flight length).

Airline won't budge on a US flight? File a free complaint with the US DOT.

Canada has proposed changes to these rules (2026) that aren't in force yet, so check before you claim. Canadian Transportation Agency: delays and cancellations

On most international trips, a treaty called the Montreal Convention may also let you claim real costs a delay caused you, up to a limit. Keep receipts and ask the airline how to claim.
