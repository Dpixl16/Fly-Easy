Pre-Departure

Know before you go.

A few minutes of prep at home saves hours of stress at the airport.

01

Check in

Check in 24 hours early

Opens as soon as your airline allows it, usually 24 hours before (some international airlines open at 48). Skip the counter, lock in your seat.

🪪 Double-check your name matches your ID

TicketALEX TRAVELER✓

A typo can get you denied at the gate.

02

Be prepared for the flight

Check your flight in the airline app

Times, gates and even whole flights can change. Look the day before and the morning of, and turn on notifications so the airline can tell you first.

02

Ask, and be friendly

Lost or unsure?

Ask airport staff. Be friendly.

Helping you is what they're there for. Start with a smile and a real "how are you doing?", stay respectful, and they'll often go out of their way for you.

😊 Friendly first🤝 Respectful always
Check-in agentsGate agentsSecurity officersInfo deskFlight crew
Delayed or cancelled? Kindness matters even more

    Smile and say helloHi! How are you doing today?

  3. 🤝
    Stay patient and respectfulEven when the answer isn't what you hoped.
  4. 💛

03

Through the airport

⏱️

It closes early. Often 30 to 60 minutes before departure. Drop your bag first, then relax.

📸

Photograph your bag and its tag. Much easier to trace if it goes missing.

💧

Empty your bottle, refill after. Liquids can't go through, but fountains wait on the other side.

🪪

ID out, pockets empty. Put your phone, keys and wallet in your bag before you reach the front.

🧭

Find your gate first, then explore. Know how long the walk is before you wander off.

🔎

Recheck the screens. Gates change after security more than you'd think, right when you've stopped looking.

🚻

Go before you board. The aisle gets crowded fast once boarding starts.

🔋

Charge up, pass ready. Top up your phone at the gate and know your boarding group.
