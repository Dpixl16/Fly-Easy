San José Mineta International

Know before you fly SJC

Silicon Valley's airport is one connected building: 36 gates, under a mile end to end, no tram. Pick your stop on the board, then open the Calm Core for the programs that make SJC one of the gentler airports to fly from.

  • Gates1–36
  • TerminalsA + B, 1 building
  • TramNot needed

    Not one room but the programs SJC runs on purpose to make the whole airport gentler. Scroll slowly and watch the window.

    01 · Therapy animals

    SJC Happy Tails Active

    The first airport therapy-animal program in the US, started after 9/11. Today more than 50 certified volunteer dogs and cats wander the terminals with their handlers, just to say hello.

    1. Keep an eye out for a volunteer handler with a dog or cat, anywhere in either terminal.
    2. Ask before you pet. Saying hi is the whole point, and a minute with a calm animal can lower a racing pulse.
    3. Evenings and weekends are your best bet. There's no fixed schedule.
    Checked Oct 2026 · flysanjose.com ↗

    02 · Hidden disabilities

    Sunflower Lanyard Free

    A green lanyard covered in sunflowers quietly tells trained staff you may need a little extra: more time, clearer directions or a quieter moment. No explanation, no proof needed.

    1. Pick one up at your airline's check-in counter or an info booth, or email info@sjc.org before you fly.
    2. Wear it from the curb to the gate, including through security.
    3. Know what it does: it gets you patience and help, not a skip to the front of the line.
    Checked Oct 2026 · flysanjose.com ↗

    03 · Sensory room

    The Nest by Nutanix Expected Winter 2026

    An 800+ sq ft sensory-friendly room for kids with autism or sensory sensitivities, designed with the Children's Discovery Museum of San Jose: calming textures, soft visuals and gentle sounds.

    1. Find it after security, between Terminals A and B, across from Gate 17.
    2. Check it's open on flysanjose.com before you count on it. It's still on the way.
    3. Until then, slow down on the A–B walkway under Invisible Skies, or in Terminal B's skylit halls.
    Breathe in…and out.
    Checked Oct 2026 · flysanjose.com ↗
