TSA basics

What trips people up most.

🧴 Liquids — 3-1-1

  • 3.4 oz max per container, 1 quart bag
  • Bigger → checked bag

🔋 Batteries & power banks

  • Never checked — carry-on only
  • 100 Wh max each

🔪 Knives & multi-tools

  • Any blade → checked only

🍪 Food & snacks

  • Solid food — either bag
  • Spreads count as liquids

👟 At the checkpoint

  • Shoes stay on
  • PreCheck: laptop & liquids stay in your bag

From TSA’s own guidance. Rules change — check tsa.gov/whatcanibring.
