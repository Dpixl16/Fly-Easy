Who · About me

Hi, I'm Kollen.

I'm a junior at Junipero Serra High School (Class of 2028), an aviation enthusiast and an aspiring pilot. I've loved airplanes for as long as I can remember, and that passion is why I created Fly Easy.

I've flown to Hong Kong to visit family almost every year since I was little, and it's still the flight I look forward to most. Closer to home, SFO is my airport, and my favorite planespotting spot is the San Francisco Airport Marriott Waterfront, looking out over runway 28L.

What · Best-kept secrets

Most travelers never hear the tips that make flying easier.

A lot of genuinely useful airport know-how never reaches the public. Without it, timing becomes guesswork: when to leave, how long security will take, how far the gate really is.

And when you're rushing, you miss the best part of the trip: the sunrise over the runway, the sunset from the gate, the aircraft itself and the view from the window seat.

How the tips are made

Every tip is checked, to the best of my ability.

I work hard to keep every tip accurate, but airports are always changing: gates move, rules get updated and new spots open, so a tip can become outdated. Always confirm the specifics before you fly.

  1. 01

    Researched

    Tips start with official sources: airport websites like flysfo.com, the TSA and the airlines themselves.

  2. 02

    Reviewed by people who know

    I cross-check details and ask aviation professionals to review them for accuracy, like Mr. Strange, who worked with Alaska Airlines at SFO. When something can't be confirmed, like parking prices, the tip links to the official source instead of guessing.

  3. 03

    Kept current

    Airports change all the time, so I update tips as soon as I learn something has changed.

  4. 04

    Shaped by the community

    Honest feedback from the aviation and travel community is one of the biggest ways Fly Easy keeps improving. Spot something wrong, know a better tip, or just want to comment? Please tell me.
