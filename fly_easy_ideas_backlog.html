Fly Easy — Feature & Idea Backlog

Ideas worth boarding

A running list of features, content, and partnerships for Fly Easy, sorted by category and checked against the two things the project exists to do. Checked directly against the live codebase on 2026-08-23 — every idea below is adjusted so nothing duplicates what's already shipped.

Pillar one

Show the beauty of aviation — the parts of flying worth slowing down for, not just enduring.

Pillar two

Reduce airport stress — practical, specific help for the moments that make travelers anxious.

What's already live, as of v0.5.0 (2026-08-22): Home, Global Tips (4-phase guide), SFO Tips (AirTrain-style terminal explorer for all 6 terminals + a 56-airline gate search), Timeblocker (backward-planning with share link, .ics export, printable PDF), and Packer — its 4-step wizard (Trip → Essentials → Bags → Review) with TSA-checked suggestions and its own printable list shipped just last week, so it's dropped from this backlog as an open item. The whole site is static HTML/CSS/JS with no backend or accounts — state lives in the URL or localStorage — and already carries a consistent "golden hour" aviation-photography identity (a scroll-based sky theme, a plane-and-clouds page transition, a planespotting filmstrip on the home page). Several terminal panels already surprised me with content worth building on — see the revised cards below, flagged already partly live.

01

Content & tips

Mostly writing, photography, and static assets — the lowest-friction way to lean into "beauty of aviation" and give anxious travelers something to read or look at instead of a countdown clock.

Golden Hour Gallery already partly live

Turn the home page's auto-scrolling filmstrip (already 8 real photos on loop) into a pausable, clickable gallery — same images, but explorable instead of just decorative.

Beauty Buildable now

Zero new assets needed — the ~40-photo library already sits in /images. This is a lightbox/interaction upgrade on content that exists today.

Plane-Spotting Field Guide already partly live

SFO Tips already has one line about the SkyTerrace deck (free, landside, on the Terminal 2 roof) — buried inside the Terminal 2 panel. Pull it out into a real guide, and add the well-known off-airport spots (Bayfront Park, the In-N-Out on the flight path) that aren't mentioned at all.

Beauty + stress Buildable now

Gives early or anxious arrivers something calming to do instead of just waiting — and gives the SkyTerrace tip the visibility it deserves.

Window-Seat Almanac

Which side of the plane gets the view on popular SFO routes — e.g. sit left SFO→JFK for the Sierras at sunset.

Beauty Buildable now

Complements, doesn't duplicate — Global Tips already has a generic "sit behind the wing" seat tip; this would be route-specific. Content only to start; could grow into a small route-lookup tool later.

Turbulence, Demystified

A plain-language explainer on why turbulence happens and why it isn't dangerous.

Stress Buildable now

One of the most common sources of in-flight anxiety — directly addressable with content.

Calm Corners, Surfaced already partly live

SFO Tips already documents a free meditation room (Berman Reflection Room, phone-free, International Terminal), two yoga rooms, the SkyTerrace, and rotating SFO Museum art galleries — but each one is buried inside its own terminal's panel in the AirTrain explorer, so a stressed traveler has to already know which terminal to click. Pull all four into one visible "quiet spaces" view.

Beauty + stress Buildable now

No new research needed — this reorganizes data that already exists in the SFO Tips terminal objects into a second, easier-to-scan view. Probably the strongest "reduce stress" idea here, precisely because the content is already accurate and already written.

Kids' Sky Corner

Printable coloring pages and simple "how planes fly" explainers for family travelers.

Beauty + stress Buildable now

Static PDFs/images — reduces stress for the family-traveler segment you already target. Could cross-link to SFO's existing kid spots (the climbable "Tree Town" structure at Gate B18, the motion-activated "Spirogyrate" floor at Gate E7) already documented in SFO Tips.

Beauty Buildable now*

Assumptions & open items

  • Tech stack — confirmed, not assumed: reviewed the live code directly on 2026-08-23. It's static HTML/CSS/JS (index/global/sfo/timeblocker/packer.html + one shared styles.css and script.js), no backend, no accounts — Timeblocker's share link, .ics export, and print/PDF, plus Packer's localStorage persistence, all confirm the client-side assumption from the first draft of this backlog was right. Every "buildable now" tag above should be trustworthy.
  • Packer has shipped (v0.5.0, 2026-08-22) — it's no longer an open item; the earlier version of this backlog and the video-tutorial plan both had it as "later." Worth revisiting the tutorial plan now that it's live.
  • Airport expansion: you're open to 2–3 more major airports. Worth picking based on where your audience actually flies — common candidates would be a big West Coast hub, a big East Coast hub, and a major connecting hub, but the right picks depend on your traffic/feedback data, not a generic list.
  • LiveATC audio: check their embed/usage terms before shipping "Sounds of the Tower."
  • TSA wait-time data: confirm what TSA actually publishes at the airport level before committing to the estimator's accuracy.
